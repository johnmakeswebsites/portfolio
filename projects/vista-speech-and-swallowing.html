Vista Speech & Swallowing

Full marketing launch, start to finish.

The brief

Vista Speech & Swallowing, a speech therapy private practice in Denver, opened in August 2026. A new practice has no reputation to trade on, so the launch had to do two jobs at once: look established, and be findable by people searching for a therapist nearby.

The work

I ran the launch end to end, from the identity through the website, the referral collateral, and the local SEO that makes the practice findable. Because those decisions were made together rather than sequentially, the way the practice presents itself and the way patients find it answer to the same brief.
