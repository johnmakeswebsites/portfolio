## Blond in Car — `projects/blond-in-car.html`

**Title tag:** Blond in Car — John Caviness
**Meta description:** Logo, vinyl packaging, and Shopify storefront for Blond in Car, a music label under Albatross Pictures.

- **Back link:** Selected work
- **Index:** 01
- **Tags:** Brand • Print • Web
- **Title:** Blond in Car
- **Subtitle:** Identity, packaging and storefront.

**What it was**
Blond in Car is a music label operating under Albatross Pictures. It needed an identity that could survive being printed on a record sleeve and shrunk to a storefront favicon, and a place to sell the records once they existed.

**What I made**
I designed the logo, the vinyl packaging, and the Shopify storefront the merch sells through — the identity and the surfaces it has to live on, rather than a mark handed off to someone else to apply.

## Vista Speech & Swallowing — `projects/vista-speech-and-swallowing.html`

**Title tag:** Vista Speech & Swallowing — John Caviness
**Meta description:** Identity, website, and local search for Vista Speech & Swallowing, a new speech therapy private practice in Denver.

- **Back link:** Selected work
- **Index:** 02
- **Tags:** Brand • Web • Search
- **Title:** Vista Speech & Swallowing
- **Subtitle:** Identity, site and local search.

**What it was**
Vista Speech & Swallowing is a speech therapy private practice opening in Denver. A new practice has no reputation to trade on yet, so the work had to do two jobs at once: look established, and be findable by people searching for a therapist nearby.

**What I made**
I built the identity, the website, and the local search presence as one piece of work, so the way the practice looks and the way it gets found were decided together rather than bolted on afterwards.
