Blond in Car

Identity, packaging, and the store it sells through.

The brief

Blond in Car is a recording artist with two albums to her name. She needed an identity that would survive both a twelve-inch record sleeve and a storefront thumbnail, a place to sell the records once they were pressed, and a site that could carry tour dates as they were announced.

The work

I designed the identity and the packaging for the 2023 debut, a run of 100 records, and built the Shopify storefront it sold through, handling shipping configuration, the presale, and the sales channels the catalogue moves across. Email and paid social carried the presale. Her website and tour date listings followed. The second album, a run of 500, ships with its release on November 4th.
