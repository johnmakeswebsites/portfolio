Aural Analytics

Rebrand lead, reporting directly to the CEO.

What it was

Aural Analytics is a clinical speech technology startup. The company had outgrown its existing brand and needed one that held up in front of clinicians, investors, and conference audiences.

What I made

I led the rebrand, then carried it through the sales collateral and the conference marketing that had to sell the company in person. I worked directly with the CEO throughout — the brand decisions and the company’s positioning were the same conversation.
