About

I'm John. I've spent a decade helping small teams punch above their weight — first in-house, now on my own.

Most of my clients come to me at an awkward moment: the thing works, people like it, but nobody can explain it in a sentence. That's the job. I dig until the sentence shows up, then build everything else around it.

I work solo on strategy and design, and pull in a small bench of writers, photographers, and developers when a project needs them. You'll always be talking to me, not an account manager.
