Vista Speech & Swallowing

Identity, site and local search.

What it was

Vista Speech & Swallowing is a speech therapy private practice opening in Denver. A new practice has no reputation to trade on yet, so the work had to do two jobs at once: look established, and be findable by people searching for a therapist nearby.

What I made

I built the identity, the website, and the local search presence as one piece of work, so the way the practice looks and the way it gets found were decided together rather than bolted on afterwards.
