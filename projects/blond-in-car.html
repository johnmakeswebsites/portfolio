Blond in Car

Identity, packaging and storefront.

What it was

Blond in Car is a music label operating under Albatross Pictures. It needed an identity that could survive being printed on a record sleeve and shrunk to a storefront favicon, and a place to sell the records once they existed.

What I made

I designed the logo, the vinyl packaging, and the Shopify storefront the merch sells through — the identity and the surfaces it has to live on, rather than a mark handed off to someone else to apply.
