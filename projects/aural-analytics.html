Aural Analytics

Rebrand lead, working between the CEO and the agency.

The brief

Aural Analytics is a clinical speech technology startup. As it moved from a small startup to a fully funded company, its branding needed to be cleaned up and formalized across the whole organization, and it needed to hold up in front of clinicians, investors, and conference audiences.

The work

I led the 2019 rebrand from inside the company, working as the link between the CEO and the marketing firm hired to execute it. I carried his direction into the brief, reviewed what came back, and made the calls on what shipped. Over the following years I built the new brand out across the sales collateral, press materials, and conference marketing, which ran through eight events from the Alzheimer’s Association International Conference in 2021 to CES in 2023.
